Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

We do not collect personal data from visitors or from TikTok users. The only TikTok account our tool connects to is our own.

This website

This site has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Lebanon Daily Publisher and TikTok

When we connect the tool to our TikTok account, TikTok gives us an access token with two permissions: basic account information (user.info.basic) and uploading videos to the account's drafts (video.upload). We use them only to upload our own videos as drafts. The token is stored on our own computer, is not shared with anyone, and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app's settings. We do not read, store or share any other TikTok user's data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
