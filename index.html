LEBANON DAILY

Lebanese history and culture, one short video a day.

Lebanon Daily makes short videos about Lebanon's history, places, language and food, with an English voiceover and Arabic subtitles. Every fact in a video is checked against at least two independent sources before it is published.

How it works

We research and produce each video ourselves. A small tool, Lebanon Daily Publisher, uploads the finished video to the drafts of our own TikTok account. A person on our team then reviews the draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it. The tool never posts on its own and does not access anyone else's account.
